## Featured Article

📌 **[Is the AI industry really ready to slow down?](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/20/is-the-ai-industry-really-ready-to-slow-down/)**  
*Published on TechCrunch*

I find this article interesting because it talks about the tension between **rapid innovation** and **governance standards** in the tech industry.
Amongst the leaders of this AI landscape, we have Anthropic's Dario Amodei who believes that new models shouldn't be pushed to the public without independent safety audits to prevent risks in terms of quality and safety. On the other side, Nvidia's Jensen Huang pushes back against this 
"artificial throttling" of the models development. This has very tangible impact on the job market for AI engineers, both for new grads and for current engineers, as this impacts the pace and depth of AI model development.
